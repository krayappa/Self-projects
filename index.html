Our Story

Born in the Malaysian Rainforest

Founded in 2018, Asher was born deep within the ancient rainforests of Borneo and Peninsular Malaysia. We partner with indigenous communities to sustainably harvest the world's rarest botanicals — from wild Tongkat Ali root to high-canopy Tualang honey.

Every formula is meticulously crafted by our team of cosmetic scientists and traditional herbalists, blending cutting-edge biotechnology with 5,000 years of rainforest botanical wisdom passed down through generations.
