Our Origin

Built from ingredients sourced where the rainforest is still alive and working.

Asher began with repeated sourcing trips through Sabah, Pahang, and the older forest edges of Peninsular Malaysia, where growers, beekeepers, and herbal processors still work close to the land. What started as ingredient research turned into long-term partnerships built around traceability, slower harvesting, and small-batch extraction.

That is why the collection centers on materials with a real place behind them: tamanu pressed near the Borneo coast, pandan gathered for its fresh green oils, Tongkat Ali selected for strength and recovery, and Tualang honey collected high above the forest floor. The goal was never to sound exotic. It was to make products that carry the climate, texture, and richness of where they come from.

Atmosphere Build

The setting is part of the product.

In Malaysia, humidity, shade, rain, and heat shape how botanicals smell, dry, and settle on the skin. The visual direction follows that same logic: darker entry, cooler mist, warmer clearings, and more contrast as the page unfolds.

It gives the collection a stronger sense of place and lets the formulas feel grounded in climate, not just packaging.

Rainforest Treasures

Ingredients from the deep Malaysian canopy.

Each ingredient was chosen for what it does on the skin first, and for the landscape it comes from second. The sourcing story matters because the performance does.

Tongkat Ali Root

Deep Borneo adaptogen used here for firmness and resilience, with a profile that feels grounded and mineral-rich.

Pandan Leaf

Sharp, green, aromatic brightness that gives the collection a very specific Southeast Asian identity.

Wild Hibiscus

Used for glow and softness, but also visually important because it introduces the rose-clay note across the whole interface.

Tualang Honey

Harvested high in the canopy. It adds warmth, glow, and the gold accent that keeps the design from becoming monotonous green.
